How To Play

A new secret word is generated every day, and it's the same for players all over the world.

Guess the word in 6 tries. Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word.

The color of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was to the word.

Once finished (win or lose), copy your scores and grid patterns using the Share Result button to share with friends!

Dordle style: solve two 5-letter words at the same time in 7 attempts.

Each word you guess is entered on both boards simultaneously.

Once a board is solved, it locks down and keyboard entries only apply to the remaining board.

Keyboard Coloring Rules

Since keys are shared, the virtual keyboard combines letter evaluations from both boards. Green (correct) takes precedence over Yellow (present), which takes precedence over Gray (absent).

e
Board 1: Correct
e
Board 2: Absent

The keyboard key E will color as Green (Correct).

Solve as many words as possible before the timer runs out!

Each word solved resets the board and gives you a new solution word.

Time Attack Rules

  • ⏱️ Starting Time: 2 minutes.
  • ✨ Points: +1 Point per correct word.
  • ➕ Bonus Time: +15 seconds per correct word (capped at 5 minutes).
  • 🔄 Key Persistence: Keyboard letter colors carry over to the next word so you don't lose track of solved letters.

Solve the word in 6 attempts, with a hint!

A dictionary definition of the secret word is displayed in the clue banner at the top of the grid. Use it to narrow down your guesses.

Multiplayer Rules

  • 🧑‍💻 Player 1: Types a secret 5-letter word. It must be a valid word from our dictionary list.
  • 🎮 Player 2: Attempts to solve the custom word in 6 attempts.

Pass the device between players during set up so that Player 2 does not see the secret solution!
